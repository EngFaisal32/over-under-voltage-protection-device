# Custom 3D enclosure

A custom enclosure was designed in **Shapr3D** for the PCB and step-down transformer.

## Design intent

- Separate PCB and transformer placement areas
- Appropriate component fit and fastening
- Organized wiring pathways
- Easier access for assembly and maintenance
- Mechanical protection and portability

The report gives an approximate PCB size of **100 × 60 mm**, with a transformer region of approximately **50 × 40 mm**.

## Design progression

1. Measure board and transformer dimensions.
2. Define separate internal regions.
3. Model an enclosure in Shapr3D.
4. Verify the intended arrangement of PCB and transformer.
5. Assemble the parts into the enclosure.
